Delivery, returns, privacy and terms

Delivery and returns

Delivery. We deliver in Greater Accra and to other parts of Ghana. At checkout you choose your region and town, and the delivery fee for that place is shown in your cart before you pay. You also pick the day and time slot you want your order delivered. We will call you to confirm.

Payment. You can pay online with card or mobile money, or pay on delivery where offered. Online payments are processed by Paystack. We never see or store your card or mobile money details.

Returns and exchanges. If your item does not fit or arrives faulty, contact us within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed and have their tags and numbered label attached. We will exchange the size or refund you. Faulty or wrong items are replaced at no cost to you.

Limited editions. Numbered limited editions are made in small runs. Once a size sells out it may not return.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to take and deliver your order: your name, phone number, delivery address, city and, if you give it, your email. If you join our list we also keep your email address.

  • We use your details to process and deliver your order, contact you about it and send receipts.
  • We use your email for drop announcements only if you signed up for them. You can ask us to remove you at any time.
  • We share your details only with the services needed to run the shop: our payment provider (Paystack), our email provider and our delivery riders.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Your cart, saved items and recently viewed products are stored in your own browser and are not sent to us until you place an order.
